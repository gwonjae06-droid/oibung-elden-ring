# Displayed acts 5–9 follow-up

- Act 5 protects the literal hand/embrace/flight/royal/brass/self-discovery
  phrases from new enemy windups. Existing strikes finish with a short physical
  follow-through; authoritative hand positions still own contact.
- Act 6 adds optional elevated reward balconies over the basic path. Rewards
  on those balconies keep their own height; obstacles cannot spawn through them.
- Act 7 repeats simple figures first, then introduces quicker steps and late
  offbeats. The leader and player keep the same source-audio contact chart.
- Act 8 shows the equipped weapon, resolves hits at extension, preserves fighter
  positions through verse changes, eases knockback and gives successful parries
  an actual retaliation window without freezing the defender.
- Act 9 replaces the credit rings with eleven timed cast incidents: original
  Kwon sword poses, clown wrist IK/mallet, rolling photo die, original volumetric
  blood crest, Gyuwon's book/pages and the four player weapon rigs. A dodge can
  expose a second actor to a hit; blocks and parries lead into later counters.
  The cinematic moves only the presentation cast and never deducts combat HP.
  Recovery returns actors to the camera before the existing ensemble photo.

Validation was deliberately limited per user request: changed-module syntax,
five earlier-act scene renders, six credit snapshots and one mobile viewport.
No full test suite, full real-time credit playback or physical mobile run was
performed. Isolated coarse credit snapshots used 804–820 draw submissions and
117,638–118,450 triangles; the new projectile props are pooled. These counts are
not a physical-device FPS claim. The original cast remains the dominant draw
cost; GPU time and before/after frame time were not measured.
